## A Layer-Two Solution for Our Fragile Hands

Human hands are soft and fragile. This project develops an external layer of protection inspired by animal armor found in nature. I studied the patterns of pangolins and fugu, then expanded on the pangolin’s scales to sketch what the glove might look like.

## The Making Process

I tried different paper-folding techniques and several ways of connecting the pieces.

![Cuts](https://shih.app/blog/assets/hands/IMG_1193-2k54xgm-1024x683.jpg)

The process was frustrating at first, but after testing many methods, I found an easier and more stable way to connect the paper scales.
